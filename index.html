Frequently Asked Questions

Glo Karts offers guided electric go-kart tours through downtown Chicago. Riders cruise iconic streets at night in glowing karts, taking in landmark views with a guide leading the way. Tours are fun, social, and perfect for all experience levels.

Click BOOK NOW from any page to open the Glo Karts booking form. Select your preferred date, tour time and number of riders.

Once your reservation is completed and confirmed, you will receive a confirmation email with your booking information.

Need help booking or have questions? Call or text (630) 329-0885 for assistance.

Each Glo Karts experience includes approximately one hour of ride time through Downtown Chicago.

We recommend arriving 15 minutes before your scheduled tour time for check-in and to make sure everyone is ready to Ride the Glo.

Want to keep the Glo going? Additional ride time may be scheduled in advance, based on availability and additional pricing.

For the safety of our guests, Glo Karts does not operate in rain or unsafe weather conditions.

If rain or unsafe weather affects your scheduled experience, your reservation will be rescheduled for another available date and time.

Glo Karts monitors weather conditions and will contact you if your reservation needs to be rescheduled.
