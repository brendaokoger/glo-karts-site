What Is Glo Karts?

Glo Karts, LLC is preparing to bring a bold new private ride experience to Chicago. With stylish street-ready golf carts, LED lighting, music, and city energy, Glo Karts is designed for tours, celebrations, nightlife, and private event transportation.

Service availability will depend on licensing approval, city requirements, weather, route restrictions, and operational readiness.
